## SEASON FORMAT
The Harvest 2026 Championship Racing Season includes six weeks of points-scoring racing, with no standalone Season Championship Final.
Each week features a public track on the official schedule.
Qualifying runs from Thursday at 9 AM PT through Monday at 9 AM PT.
Racers may run the qualifying track as many times as they want. Only their fastest time counts.
The top 24 fastest drivers qualify for that week’s competition.
Weekly points accumulate across all six weeks. The racer with the most points at the end of the season is the Harvest champion.
The top 12 racers in the final Harvest standings qualify for the Harvest semifinal at Genesis Week 2027 on Paris.
Week 5 is Bonus Points Week. The bonus point boost will be announced before Week 5 qualifying begins.
Week 6 is open through the same weekly qualifying process as the other weeks; it is not restricted to the season’s top 12.
At Genesis Week, racers compete in each seasonal semifinal they have qualified for. Points earned in those semifinals determine the top six racers who advance to the Grand Final. Genesis Week includes five races total, including the Grand Final.
Weekly Race Settings and Advancement

Max Performance Score Cap: All Championship races are limited to MPS 260.
Public Races: All qualifying and competition races must be set to public.
Qualifying Phase:Thursday at 9 AM PT through Monday at 9 AM PT on the designated weekly track.
Eliminations: The six slowest qualifiers are eliminated. The top 18 advance.
Weekly Semifinals: The 18 advancing racers are split into three balanced heats based on qualifying times:
Heat 1: Positions 1, 6, 7, 12, 13, and 18
Heat 2: Positions 2, 5, 8, 11, 14, and 17
Heat 3: Positions 3, 4, 9, 10, 15, and 16
Weekly Final Heat: The top two finishers from each weekly semifinal advance to the final heat, for a total of six racers.
Weekly Final Rankings: All 24 racers are ranked across the three race phases:
Positions 1 through 6 are based on final heat results.
Positions 7 through 18 are based on semifinal race times.
Positions 19 through 24 are based on qualifying times.

### POWER-UPS AND MPS

Power-ups are included in a racer’s total Max Performance Score.
Racers may choose a higher-performance vehicle with fewer boosts or a lower-MPS vehicle that leaves more room for power-ups.
The stronger power-up boosts introduced in Sizzle remain part of Championship Racing. Racers are responsible for testing their boosts and understanding their effect before competition.
Power-ups may be purchased on the secondary market and are also available in the Upland Store every Thursday.
